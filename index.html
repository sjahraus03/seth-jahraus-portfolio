About

A reporter built on curiosity

Hi there, I'm Seth Jahraus, a multimedia journalist from Kansas City. I like to tell the stories of the people affected by the news of the day. I founded and currently produce the podcast Colorado 1st, covering the underreported issues affecting Coloradans.

Before that, I studied at Colorado College where — after deciding I wasn't cut out to be a doctor — I majored in English Creative Writing and minored in journalism. I reported for the school paper and worked for Rocky Mountain PBS, winning awards for my work at both outlets.

Outside journalism, I've played soccer and guitar all my life and have found reprieve from the day-to-day in both. I also participate in Moth StorySLAMs when I can, where I like to shine a comedic light on my failures. I'm a staunch Chiefs supporter, and yes, it's a big part of my personality.
